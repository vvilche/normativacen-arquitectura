Dear Members of the Managing Board, 
 
Munoz Munoz Limitada (“Munoz”) is the authorized representative of Manitoba Hydro 
International Ltd. (“MHI”) in Chile. MHI is the developer and owner of PSCAD™ and has a proven 
track record in electromagnetic transient (EMT)–based modeling and simulation, supporting 
utilities and system operators worldwide in system planning and operational studies. 
 
In April 2024, MHI and Munoz had the opportunity to meet with CEN engineering experts to 
discuss EMT simulations, highlight the importance of using vendor-level, real-code EMT models, 
and offer support from MHI. As part of that conversation, we also highlighted how PSCAD is widely 
used by vendors to create detailed models that accurately reflect the performance of their power 
electronics devices, including, without limitation, high-voltage direct current (“HVDC”), wind, solar, 
battery energy storage systems (“BESS”), and others.  
 
Although we have not had further discussions after the 2024 meeting, we remain available to 
support the development of a wide-area model in PSCAD, enabling original equipment 
manufacturer (“OEM”) models to be readily included in critical simulations. 
 
We learned of the recent Chilean blackout. We recognize that events of this nature can pose 
significant challenges when attempting to replicate them for future grid studies. In light of this, we 
emphasize the importance of using real-code models, which provide a robust framework for 
accurately analyzing complex system behaviours. 
 
We remain fully committed to supporting the Chilean energy system in every way possible, and we 
welcome guidance from CEN on if and how we may contribute. PSCAD continues to be a valuable 
tool for developers and plant owners, particularly those facing oscillations that require deeper 
investigation beyond what generic models can offer.   Real-code OEM models play a critical role in 
these cases, and we hope to collaborate in ways that reinforce their value, while supporting 
broader goals of system reliability and resilience. 
 
CEN holds PSCAD licenses, and as a valued customer, we would like to share the following outline 
of services, which MHI would be pleased to discuss, should these be of interest to CEN: 
• 
Conversion of the entire transmission network system into PSCAD format, including: 
o 
Integration of dynamic models into the network, benchmarked and tested for 
accuracy.
